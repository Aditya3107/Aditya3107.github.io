I am a PhD researcher in speech technology and language learning, with a focus on **Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)**, **Mispronunciation Detection and Diagnosis (MDD)**, and **Computer-Assisted Pronunciation Training (CAPT)**.

I am broadly interested in building robust, interpretable speech systems that can support second-language learners, helping learners receive meaningful, fine-grained feedback on their speech. My work draws on deep learning, acoustic-phonetic modeling, and human-in-the-loop evaluation to bridge the gap between speech research and real-world language education.
